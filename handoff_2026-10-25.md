## The main thread: gan_bpm's λ_ext sweep (step 1), then its adversarial fine-tune (step 2)

**Running now:** `condor/ext_sweep.dag` from `gan_bpm_next`: rCBF, λ_adv = 0, λ_ext ∈ {50 (the
control), 200, 500}, fold 0, 40 epochs with early stopping off, a trial of each then a pilot
(about 5 h each). Outputs: `/radraid2/colivares/matrix/gan_bpm_rcbf_f13uclav2_adv0_ext<λ>_e40_{trial,f0}/`.

**Built, not run:** step 2, the ESRGAN-style fine-tune (gan_bpm `ext_gan_plan.md` §2.7b,
commit `c168304`). It starts from step 1's pick for each fold, warms the discriminator up for
500 steps, holds the adversary at a set share of the generator's gradient (VQGAN's adaptive
weight; 2A runs shares 0.2 and 0.5 under WGAN-GP), and logs health columns every epoch. All of
it is inert at its defaults, so the running sweep is unaffected, even after a `git pull` in
`gan_bpm_next`.

### 3. Put the pick into `finetune.dag`, and submit

In `/raid/colivares/github_repos/gan_bpm_next/condor/finetune.dag`, set the one line to the
picked folder's **name** (the job adds `/radraid2/colivares/matrix/`):

```

```

If the summary says `PICK: none`, use the control, `gan_bpm_rcbf_f13uclav2_adv0_ext50_e40_f0`.
The job refuses the placeholder. Then:

```bash
cd /raid/colivares/github_repos/gan_bpm_next/condor && condor_submit_dag -maxjobs <free GPUs> finetune.dag
```

Two 2-epoch trials, then the two pilots (WGAN-GP at shares 0.2 and 0.5, fold 0, 20 epochs).
Each job's `.out` ends with its health table: per epoch, the adversary's share over the whole
generator and at its last layer, its direction against the pixel losses, the λ_adv used, and the
critic's real − fake gap. **Healthy** (§2.4): the share within 0.5–2× its target in at least 80 %
of epochs, the gap neither running away nor collapsing.

### 4. When the fine-tune pilots finish: score them against their own start

The same core-check command, with step 1's pick **first** (the control of §2.6), then the two
fine-tunes (`…_ft_wgan_s0p2_ext<λ>_e20_f0`, `…_ft_wgan_s0p5_ext<λ>_e20_f0`). A fine-tune wins if
its Dice at the cutoff beats its start with a 95 % CI above 0, its predicted core is at most
1.25 × the label's, its matched Dice (place) doesn't drop, and its brain MAE is at most 25 % up.
After that, per the plan's §2.5: 2B (the relativistic arm) or 2C (feature matching) with
`condor_submit finetune_cell.sub -append …`, 2D (`scripts/interpolate_weights.py`), and 2E (all
5 folds of the winner).

## Today's findings (decisions #26–#29 here; D-98, D-99 in gan_bpm)

- **ISLES 2018, rCBF folds 0 and 1, not yet paired:** DWI Dice 0.083 on parenchyma per scan,
  against the label's 0.238 and gan_bpm's all-data models' 0.034–0.109 (λ_adv = 0 `epoch_9`
  0.036). MRDPM calls more core (median 6–6.5 mL against gan_bpm's 1.4–4.3; the label's 21.8),
  but its low-rCBF bias (+0.28) and spread (0.81) are gan_bpm's: the diffusion stage didn't undo
  the squeeze. MAE 0.268 against about 0.25.
- **Three fold models trained unseen** (#26): Condor restarted Tmax fold 3 and rCBF folds 3 and 4
  from scratch into folders the copy step skipped. `condor/ctp_only_recover_runs.sub` copies them
  (Carlos ran it; its closing table wasn't reviewed here). A restarted training job now resumes
  from its own newest checkpoint.
- **The CV folds are a development set from now on** (#29); ISLES 2018 stays untouched.
- **Why the core is missed, gan_bpm's side** (the CV held-out cases, against the label's own
  rCBF < 0.3 core): Dice 0.42 at the cutoff, 0.48 with the cutoff removed, 0.06 for random voxels,
  predicted volume 0.59 × the label's. **Mostly a place problem**; a better cutoff (0.4) gains
  only about 0.04. Small cores are the worst (0.25 at 5–15 mL).
- **L_ext's share of gan_bpm's step** at the fold models' `best.pt` (epochs 7–16 of 40): 6–20 %
  (30 % at initialization), L_pix 80–94 %; in three folds it pushes in a direction L_pix doesn't.
